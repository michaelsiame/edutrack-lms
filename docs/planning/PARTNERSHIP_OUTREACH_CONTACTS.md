## 1. International Certification Partners (Priority)

### CompTIA Authorized Partner Program
- **What you get:** Official CompTIA curriculum, badges, marketing materials, discounted exam vouchers, and the right to promote courses as CompTIA-aligned.
- **Application portal:** https://partner.comptia.org/
- **Program info:** https://www.comptia.org/membership/it-career-center/authorized-partner-program
- **Contact (general partner inquiries):** Use the web form at https://www.comptia.org/contact-us or the partner portal chat.
- **Tip:** Apply as an **Academic/Training Partner**. Have your TEVETA registration certificate and college accreditation documents ready.

### Cisco Networking Academy
- **What you get:** Free Cisco Networking Academy platform, curricula (CCNA, CyberOps, IoT, Python, etc.), instructor training, badges, and global recognition.
- **Become an Academy:** https://www.netacad.com/institutions/become-academy
- **Academy support email:** academysupport@external.cisco.com
- **Alternative contact:** Use the "Contact Us" form at https://www.netacad.com/contact-us
- **Tip:** A TEVETA-registered vocational college qualifies. Identify at least one instructor who will complete the free Cisco instructor training.

### Huawei ICT Academy (Zambia already has a Huawei/Smart Zambia relationship)
- **What you get:** HCIA/HCIP/HCIE certification tracks, free course materials, lab simulators, instructor certification (HCAI), exam vouchers, and access to the Huawei ICT Competition.
- **Academy portal:** https://e.huawei.com/en/talent/ict-academy
- **Instructor certification email:** certification@huawei.com
- **Local route:** Contact **Huawei Technologies Zambia** directly. Because Huawei and Smart Zambia already partner on national ICT training, mention that alignment and ask to be onboarded as a vocational academy partner.
- **Tip:** If you have a Huawei-certified instructor or plan to certify one, lead with that.
